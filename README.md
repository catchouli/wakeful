# wakeful

An RPG about identity and finding yourself.

You wake with nothing — no name, no past, no idea who you're meant to be.
*wakeful* is a game about piecing that together: who you decide to be, and
who you find yourself becoming along the way.

## Status

Very early in development — nothing playable yet.
